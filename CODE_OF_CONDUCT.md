# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers must be respectful, constructive and welcoming.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and sharing private
information without consent are unacceptable in issues, reviews and discussions.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or close contributions to
protect participants. Reports can be sent privately through GitHub's reporting
mechanisms or to a repository maintainer; avoid posting private incident details
in a public issue. Maintainers should assess context fairly and protect reporter
privacy. This policy applies to project spaces and representation of the project.
